Find the box

Orwell Terrace police box, right at the Telfer Subway in Dalry. Postcode EH11 2DD. Near Fountain Park and Dalry. Short walk from Haymarket. Near the cycle and walk way to Roseburn.

Stop by and say hi while visiting Dalry or walking through the Telfer Subway. Takeaway from the hatch.

The police box, now Pourboy

Edinburgh put up hundreds of these in the 1930s. Each was a street point with a telephone, a wee room for an officer, and a lamp on top. A lot of them now sell coffee. This one is Pourboy. Sam is inside. Not a Police Scotland box any more.

Espresso to take away, and baked cookies when Sam has them. Today’s tray is on Instagram.
