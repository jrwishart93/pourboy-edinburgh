Find the box

Orwell Terrace, Dalry, EH11 2DD, at the Telfer Subway. Near Fountain Park. Short walk from Haymarket. Near the cycle and walk way to Roseburn.

The police box, now Pourboy

Edinburgh put up hundreds of these in the 1930s. Street point, telephone, wee room. A lot now sell coffee. This one is Pourboy. Not a Police Scotland box any more.
